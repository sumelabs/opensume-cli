# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests, reviews or anywhere else it gathers, is expected to make it a welcoming, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their background or identity.

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It describes the behavior we expect, the behavior we do not accept, and how maintainers enforce it.

## In short

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, or publishing others' private information.
- Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may ban people who do so repeatedly.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at support@sume.com with "code of conduct" in the subject. Every report is reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.
